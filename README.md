# -inventory-forecasting-sim

**Project:** Does a forecast-based inventory policy beat a simple rule?

**Scenario:** A hypothetical 10-store convenience chain in Bangkok stocking 50 packaged beverage products. Sales data comes from a public store-item dataset and is not real Thai sales.

**Question:** Compared with a simple baseline ordering rule, does a forecast-driven policy reduce stockouts and total cost?

**Method:** SQL for data storage and cleaning, Python for forecasting and inventory simulation, and a dashboard for results. Policies are tested on a held-out final 12 months.

**Assumptions:** Holding cost, stockout cost, and lead time are assumed values.

**Data:** Store Item Demand Forecasting Challenge (Kaggle).

**Status:** In progress.
